Version 1.2 — November 2026

DOI of this version: 10.5281/zenodo.24390767.

New title: The Method. The Heart in Immune and Rare Diseases: Understanding Evidence, Building Knowledge. The texts discussed have been reread in full text, obtained through legal channels. Author name: Brahim Abdelouaeb throughout.

Corrections of accuracy

  • 1.4.2 (Frantz): “metabolic” becomes “diabetic,” as in the source.
  • 7.3.1 (Duewell): the result concerns early atherosclerosis.
  • 13.3.1 (Gillmore): the 100% specificity and positive predictive value (CI 98 to 100%) apply to all 1,217 patients.
  • 15.3.1 (Johnson): deaths added, five versus one.
  • 16.3.1 (Ferreira): the AUC* indicator is defined in a table note, not in the appendix.
  • 16.3.3 (Grayson): “71 observations” rather than “71 scans,” as in the source.
  • 17.3.2 and 17.3.3 (Agbor-Enoh, Khush): positive predictive value (19.6%), area under the curve (0.64; CI 0.52 to 0.75) and the denominator of avoided biopsies (751 of 923, six undetected rejections) added.
  • 17.3.4: the pAMR1 grade comes from the 2013 ISHLT formulation, later than the document discussed in 17.3.1.
  • 18.3.2 (Rothman): “80%” instead of “most.”
  • 18.3.3 (Yoshikawa): the significant rise in ejection fraction without treatment in the delayed group (19.5% to 22.3%, p = 0.0343) added.
  • 19.3.1 (Pujades-Rodriguez): the hazard ratio from the abstract (1.74; CI 1.64 to 1.84) added; title harmonized in the index.
  • 20.3.1 (Nielsen): the 83,315 participants are those of checkpoint inhibitor trials, not all exposed; incidences (0.80% and 1.07%) added.
  • References: issue numbers added (Yoshikawa, Pujades-Rodriguez, ADVOCATE); date of the ADVOCATE retraction (June 29, 2026).
