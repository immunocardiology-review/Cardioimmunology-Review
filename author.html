Biographie en français

Dr. Brahim Abdelouaeb is the author of the French treatise Cardioimmunologie, of La Méthode (available in English as The Method) and of Le Bilan (English edition, The Labs, in preparation), and the editor of Cardioimmunology Review. His editorial work connects immune mechanisms, clinical medicine and the critical appraisal of evidence.
