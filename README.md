## Interactive field atlas

The circular constellation keeps the heart at its centre. Its themes are organised into sensing and mediators, immune cells, regulation and repair, clinical phenotypes, organ interfaces, and observation and translation. Each theme opens a non-modal reading panel with an overview, cardiac context, evidence limits, related themes and references. On narrow screens, the map and reading panel share the available height.

Theme links use `?theme=complement#field`. The welcome and collection screens remain available through the site navigation. The book and both article language versions include responsive reading layouts and keyboard navigation.

The atlas is an educational synthesis. It does not replace the full references or disease-specific clinical guidance. Its teaching families describe a reading structure, not a single causal pathway. Bibliographic identities and access routes were checked on 19 October 2026; the book PDF and original article texts have been preserved.

The catalogue is in `assets/field-atlas-data.json`; versioned source data, a coverage audit, figures and a bilingual method are in `assets/atlas/`. The [editorial charter](https://cardioimmunologyreview.org/charte-editoriale.html) and [atlas method](https://cardioimmunologyreview.org/constellation-method.html) document responsibilities, selection and limits. Shared identity assets live in `assets/`: the engraved journal seal and responsive identity styles. The readable journal name accompanies the seal in headers and signatures; the constellation and publication palettes are preserved. Existing PDF, share image and article content are preserved.
