The author

Dr. Brahim Abdelouaeb is a general practitioner in Paris whose practice includes cardiac imaging. He is the author of the French treatise Cardioimmunologie, of La Méthode (in English, The Method) and of Le Bilan, and the editor of Cardioimmunology Review.
