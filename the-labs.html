About the book

A test is ordered for a reason, a sample is collected at the right time and a result is read alongside its source.

When an autoimmune, autoinflammatory or rare disease affects the heart, we set out to make laboratory testing readable. Each test here is assigned a tier: essential for a defined indication, useful depending on the clinical context, or a matter for specialist assessment. The tier states why the test is ordered.

We begin with how to read a laboratory workup, from specimen collection to the meaning of a result, then work through the diseases, cardiac involvement and monitoring during treatment. Each threshold and monitoring interval is linked to its source, and each chapter cross-references the Treatise.
