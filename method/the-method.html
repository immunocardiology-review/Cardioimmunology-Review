About the book

The Method is Book 2 of the Cardioimmunology collection. It examines how knowledge is built in cardioimmunology: how a result supports a proposition, how a cause is established, how sources are read and evidence is constructed.

It also shows how a conclusion can be kept, restricted or revised. The book moves from the foundations of medical knowledge to inflammation and experimental models; then to the testing of hypotheses through treatments, including the lessons of negative trials; to the connections between systems and measurements; and finally to the formation and transmission of medical judgment.

One hundred scientific texts are commented on, among them a methodological analysis of the 2025 guidelines of the European Society of Cardiology. Each commentary distinguishes what is established, what remains plausible and what cannot be concluded.

Four readings for each text

Critical reading

What can the comparison show?

Measures, bias, uncertainty

Immunological reading

What is the explanation worth?

Mechanism, context, alternatives

Cardiological reading

What does it mean for the heart?

Diagnosis, prognosis, treatment

Integrative clinical reading

What does it change for the person?

Priorities, other diseases, care pathway

A judgment shared by all four readings: keep, restrict or revise the conclusion.

This is a book of method. It is neither a professional society guideline nor an enforceable standard; current guidelines remain the reference for therapeutic decisions. Examples presented as fictional are entirely fictional.
