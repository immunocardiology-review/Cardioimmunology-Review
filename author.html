The author

Dr Brahim Abdelouaeb

General practitioner · Cardiac imagingAuthor and editor

Dr Brahim Abdelouaeb is the author of Cardioimmunologie, The Method and Le Bilan, and the editor of Cardioimmunology Review, an independent, open-access publication. A general practitioner based in Paris whose clinical work includes cardiac imaging, he explores the relationships between the heart, immunity and the critical appraisal of evidence. His books and articles are intended for medical students and physicians.
