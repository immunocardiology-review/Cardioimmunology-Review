The author

Dr Brahim Abdelouaeb

Physician · Cardiac imagingAuthor and editor

Dr Brahim Abdelouaeb is a physician, author and editor based in Paris. He is the author of the Cardioimmunologie treatise, La Méthode (available in English as The Method) and Le Bilan, and the editor of Cardioimmunology Review. A general practitioner whose clinical work includes cardiac imaging, he also develops workshops in cardio-immunological expertise. His editorial work connects immune mechanisms, clinical medicine and the critical appraisal of evidence.
