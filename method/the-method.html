About the book

The Method is the second book of the Cardioimmunology collection, after the treatise. It examines how knowledge is built in cardioimmunology: how a result supports a proposition, how a cause is established, how sources are read and evidence is constructed. It also shows how a conclusion can be kept, restricted or revised.

Twenty-one chapters in five parts move from the foundations of medical knowledge to inflammation and experimental models, the testing of hypotheses through treatments, including the lessons of negative trials, the connections between systems and measurements and the formation and transmission of medical judgment.

One hundred scientific texts are commented on, among them a methodological analysis of the 2025 guidelines of the European Society of Cardiology. Each commentary distinguishes what is established, what remains plausible and what cannot be concluded.

Four readings for each text

Critical reading
What does the comparison allow?
Measures, bias, uncertainty
Immunological reading
What is the explanation worth?
Mechanism, context, alternatives
Cardiological reading
What scope for the heart?
Diagnosis, prognosis, treatment
Integrative clinical reading
What does it change for the person?
Priorities, other diseases, care pathway
Judgment common to the four readings: keep, restrict or revise the conclusion.

Status of the text

This is a book of method. It is neither a guideline of a learned society nor an enforceable standard. The current guidelines of learned societies remain the sole authority for therapeutic decisions. Examples presented as fictional are entirely fictional.
