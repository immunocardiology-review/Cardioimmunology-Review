A critical reading of evidence

The Review offers students and physicians articles on the interfaces between cardiology and immunology. The texts distinguish observations, experimental findings and proposed interpretations.

Each article provides its references, a disclosure of interests and a statement on the use of artificial intelligence. The editorial charter sets out the rules for review, corrections and versions.

Schedule

The Review aims for a monthly schedule. An issue is announced only when its preparation and review are sufficiently advanced.
