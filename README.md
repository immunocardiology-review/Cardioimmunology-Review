## Interactive field atlas

The circular constellation keeps the heart at its centre. Its themes are organised into signals and sensing, cells and regulation, tissue responses, clinical interfaces and cross-organ connections. Each theme opens a non-modal reading panel with an overview, cardiac context, evidence limits, related themes and references. On narrow screens, the map and reading panel share the available height.

Theme links use `?theme=complement#field`. The welcome and collection screens remain available through the site navigation. The book and both article language versions include responsive reading layouts and keyboard navigation.

The atlas is an educational synthesis. It does not replace the full references or disease-specific clinical guidance. Its teaching families describe a reading structure, not a single causal pathway. Sources were checked on 15 October 2026; the book PDF and original article texts have been preserved.

Published HTML pages are self-contained: the interface styles and atlas scripts are embedded, so no additional asset upload is required for this release. Existing PDF, share image and article content are preserved.
